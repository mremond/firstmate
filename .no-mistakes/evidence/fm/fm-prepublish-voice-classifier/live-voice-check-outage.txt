### service unreachable (HTTPS proxy that drops every connection; counts connection attempts)
$ HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:<port> FM_HOME=<home with key> bin/fm-voice-check.sh --kind intent clean.md
voice-check:
  status: unverified
  kind: intent
  reason: service unavailable: http 000 after 343 ms
voice-check: unverified (service unavailable: http 000 after 343 ms); do not publish this text without an explicit instruction
exit=3
service connection attempts: 2

### same outage, explicit per-publication override with reason

  kind: intent
  reason: service unavailable: http 000 after 51 ms
