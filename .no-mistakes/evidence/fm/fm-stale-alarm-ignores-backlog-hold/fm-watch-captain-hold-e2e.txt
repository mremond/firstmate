End-to-end watcher evidence
===========================

Command:
  bin/fm-test-run.sh tests/fm-watch-triage.test.sh

This focused behavior suite launches the real bin/fm-watch.sh, creates its
backlog through tasks-axi, and opens/releases captain calls through
bin/fm-captain-hold.sh. The assertions inspect durable wake-queue entries and
whether the watcher exits to surface an alarm or remains in its poll loop.

Observed behavior:
  ok - work under an open captain call surfaces once, absorbs pane churn, then re-surfaces when the window elapses
  ok - a stale window with no open captain call keeps alarming on every new hash
  ok - a wake that never reached the durable queue arms no re-surface throttle
  ok - a released-then-re-held task is a distinct captain call whose first sight still alarms
